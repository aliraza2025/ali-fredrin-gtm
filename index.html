Selected work

    Worked with the owner, sales staff and front desk to understand estimating, then personally built and deployed an AI-assisted Next.js quoting application using Housecall Pro history and property data. Processed 11,482 historical jobs. Combined historical lookups, regression, labor estimates and business rules with explanations, confidence indicators and staff corrections.   2. CrediClip - let failed checks change the roadmap.

That redirected work toward ingestion and separating heavy workers from the web app. The final numerical score is deterministic, not produced by an LLM. Read the CrediClip case study.
